=== /legal-affairs ===
title: Legal Affairs | VOYCE
h1 The Legal Affairs Commission
h4 The Legal Affairs Commission consists of young law professionals and students from diverse European backgrounds, united by a passion for legal research, justice, and international law. We tackle a wide range of legal topics affecting the EU, with the goal of creating a platform where the legal challenges impacting European citizens can be addressed and resolved. We focus on critical areas such as the legitimacy of the European Union, the supremacy of EU law, and the alignment of EU’s actions with its foundational Treaties. By addressing these issues, we aim to reinforce legal certainty and accountability. Our commission is dedicated to ensuring that all EU policies and legal frameworks are grounded in law, promoting respect for human rights and safeguarding the rights of individuals.
